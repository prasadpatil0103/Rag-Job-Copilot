Job Posting Title

Sr. Software Engineer (Data Engineer) - Clinical Vendor Solutions Remote

Job Description

The Clinical Vendor Solutions Data Engineer plays a key role in designing, developing, testing, and maintaining the data integration solutions that power Prime’s clinical vendor hub, Partner IQ. This role ensures seamless data flow between clinical vendors and internal systems, enabling timely, accurate, and high‑quality data that supports Prime’s clinical programs and strategic initiatives.

Responsibilities
• Design, develop, test, and maintain data integration solutions that support Prime’s clinical vendor hub, Partner IQ.
• Ensure seamless data flow between clinical vendors and internal systems.
• Enable timely, accurate, and high‑quality data delivery to support Prime’s clinical programs and strategic initiatives.

Minimum Qualifications
• Bachelor's degree in computer science or related area of study, or equivalent combination of education and/or relevant work experience; High School Diploma or GED equivalent required
• 5 years of experience with application development or package software implementation
• Ability to manage work direction and lead, influence, mentor, and provide feedback to others within team
• Must be eligible to work in the United States without need for work visa or residency sponsorship

Additional Qualifications
• SQL (advanced proficiency)
• Python (primary data engineering language for building PIQ pipelines)
• GCP – Cloud Computing
• BigQuery (data warehouse, query optimization, partitioning, clustering, data modeling)
• Airflow (author, schedule & monitor workflows) – Cloud Composer
• Dataflow (data processing – batch & stream) or similar (Apache Beam)
• Ability to solve complex problems
• Ability to effectively communicate within & across multiple teams (IT, Business, Clients & Vendors)

Preferred Qualifications
• Data analysis & management (architecture, modeling, mapping, testing, optimization, etc.)
• Cloud Storage (bucket management, lifecycle policies, data archival)
• Bash/Shell (automation & scripting)
• Monitoring (cloud logging & monitoring)
• API & service integrations across platforms & applications (on-prem, cloud, SaaS)
• CICD pipelines - GitHub (continuous integration continuous deployments) or similar
• IAM frameworks & tools (identify & data access management across environments)
• Container platform & tools (Kubernetes, Docker, etc.)
• Experience across multiple OS & DB environments
• SDLC delivery within large project & ongoing support environments across varying methodologies (software development lifecycle)
• Ability to mentor team members PBM and/or Healthcare experience
