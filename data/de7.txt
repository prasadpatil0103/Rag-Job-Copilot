Role Overview

We are seeking a senior‑level Data Engineer with SAP experience, ideally with exposure to the SAP Plant Maintenance (PM) module, to support an enterprise client in the utilities / renewable energy space. This role will focus on extracting, modeling, and enabling analytics from SAP PM data while partnering closely with SAP functional and data teams.

The ideal candidate combines strong data engineering fundamentals, prior SAP experience, and the ability to quickly ramp into new SAP domains while guiding and enabling client data engineers.

What You’ll Do
• Design, build, and maintain data pipelines sourcing data from SAP, with a primary focus on SAP PM.
• Partner with SAP PM functional analysts to translate maintenance concepts and requirements into analytics‑ready data models.
• Extract, transform, and model SAP PM data for reporting and operational insights.
• Ensure data quality, reliability, and performance across SAP‑sourced datasets.
• Provide technical guidance and enablement to client data engineers, including best practices and documentation.
• Collaborate with cross‑functional teams to deliver high‑impact data solutions in a consulting environment.

Required Qualifications
• Strong experience as a Data Engineer on enterprise data platforms.
• Prior hands‑on experience working with SAP data (ECC and/or S/4HANA).
• Advanced SQL skills and experience with analytical data modeling.
• Ability to work closely with SAP functional teams and business stakeholders.
• Experience mentoring, enabling, or upskilling other data engineers.
• Strong communication skills and comfort in a client‑facing role.

Preferred Qualifications
• Direct experience with SAP PM (Plant Maintenance).
• Background in utilities, energy, or renewable energy industries.
• Experience supporting SAP analytics or reporting use cases.
• Willingness to travel to Florida 50%

Why This Role

This is a high‑impact, short‑term engagement for a senior Data Engineer who enjoys working at the intersection of SAP, data, and analytics, quickly ramping into new domains, and helping client teams become more self‑sufficient.
