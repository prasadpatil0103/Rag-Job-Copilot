Lead Data Engineer - REMOTE

This position is posted by Jobgether on behalf of a partner company. We are currently looking for a Principal Data Engineer. You will be tasked with designing and optimizing data pipelines that handle clinical data from various sources while ensuring compliance with healthcare standards. As an integral part of our engineering team, you'll have the opportunity to enhance the quality and efficiency of healthcare delivery by leveraging your expertise in both EHR systems and cloud technologies. Your contributions will directly impact the lives of thousands by facilitating seamless access to vital healthcare data.

• Develop frameworks and internal tooling for accelerating new data source onboarding.

• Strong communication skills for technical and non-technical audiences.
